# Anthro Energy

> Anthro Energy is a battery materials company in Alameda, California developing next-generation
> electrolyte technology. Its Proteus platform is a drop-in electrolyte that increases energy density,
> improves safety, and adds mechanical integrity to lithium-ion and next-gen battery cells without
> changes to existing gigafactory manufacturing infrastructure. Anthro Energy is a portfolio company
> of Union Square Ventures and Ultratech Capital. It is a physical-materials company and does not
> publish a public API, developer portal, SDKs, or machine-readable API artifacts.
